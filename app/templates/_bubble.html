{# Kaagaz — bubble characters. A cast of soft, rounded, friendly people used as illustration — never as icons and never carrying meaning on their own. They appear beside content they belong to: the person at the counter, the person handing over a file, the person who has just remembered something. These are a deliberately different layer from _icons.html. The icon set is fully outlined at a single 1.75 stroke weight and is used for regulatory buckets, where a glyph has to be unambiguous at 16px. These are filled and shaded, and they are decorative. Mixing the two styles in one place is what makes an interface look assembled rather than designed, so they never are. House rules: • 200x200 viewBox for every character, so they scale interchangeably. • One warm highlight per form, matching the CSS sheen. • Faces are the same three marks everywhere: two eyes and one smile arc. • Skin and hair tones stay in a narrow warm range; clothing carries the brand palette so the cast reads as one family. #} {# ── Shared face, so every character has the same expression ────────── #} {% macro _face(cx=100, cy=98, r=3.2) -%} {%- endmacro %} {% macro _blush(cx=100, cy=110) -%} {%- endmacro %} {% macro _shadow(cx=100, cy=180, rx=48) -%} {%- endmacro %} {# A soft vertical gradient is what gives a body volume.
